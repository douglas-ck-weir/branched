How to play
1
You're shown a start and finish station on a 5×5 grid.
2
Drag from the start square through every square on the grid, without lifting your finger, until it's completely full.
3
Station squares only accept your path when they're next in line order. Get it wrong and the square bounces back.
4
Fill every square, ending on finish, and you've solved it.
Hard mode
Two Underground lines cross this grid. Nothing tells you where: spotting the interchange station, and which stations belong to which line, is the whole puzzle.
Hints
3 free hints per puzzle. Each reveals the next square you need, but only if your current path can still be completed; if you've drawn into a dead end, it'll tell you to undo instead.
Two puzzles a day, Easy and Hard, refreshing at midnight. The same puzzles for everyone. Compare your time on the leaderboard.
